About Dr. Lane

Dr. Avery Lane is a cardiologist in Columbus, Ohio, who has cared for adults with heart disease for eighteen years. Her practice centers on prevention: finding risk early, explaining it plainly, and building a plan each patient can keep.

She trained in internal medicine and cardiovascular disease at Lakeshore University Hospital, where she served as chief resident. Today she leads the heart rhythm clinic at Central Ohio Regional Medical Center and teaches residents in the internal medicine program.

Dr. Lane has written on atrial fibrillation, cardiac rehabilitation and heart disease in women, for medical journals and for patients. She sees patients in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
