What a professional press release is

A short news style article about a real development in your career, published under a dated headline. It gives people searching your name a current, accurate account of what you are doing now, in your own approved words.

Paid publishing, not news coverage

Releases are published on The Dynamo Times, a publication of Top Dynamos. They are paid and company controlled. They are not independent editorial coverage, and they do not mean a journalist chose to write about you.

Good reasons for a release

A release works when there is something true and current to say.

  • A new position or practice

    Joining a group, opening a practice, making partner or taking a leadership role.

  • Published or presented work

    A book, a study, a major article or an invited talk.

  • A career milestone

    Decades in practice, a retirement, a new focus or a return to clinical work.

  • Service and leadership

    Board roles, military service, pro bono work or community programs.

How a release is made

  1. 1You provide the facts

    What happened, when, and why it matters, plus your CV and a photo. For the first release, we build the story with you on a call.

  2. 2We write it

    A headline, a summary and a full release in news style, about your work, in plain language.

  3. 3We check every fact

    Names, titles, dates, credentials and organizations are checked against your documents and public sources.

  4. 4You approve it

    You read the full text and approve it in writing. Nothing goes out before that.

  5. 5It is published

    The release goes live on The Dynamo Times with your photo, dated the day it goes live, and is linked from your website and profile.

What every release contains

  1. Headline
  2. Summary line
  3. Dateline: city and date
  4. Opening paragraph: who, what and when
  5. Background from your career
  6. A quote you approve
  7. About the person
  8. Your photo

What a release can and cannot do

It can give people searching your name a current, accurate page, link to your website and profile, and give you something to share with patients, clients and colleagues.

It cannot promise a search position, remove other results, or stand in for independent news coverage. Results depend on search engines we do not control.

Releases as part of reputation management

Questions

Where is my release published?

On The Dynamo Times, a publication of Top Dynamos. Any other distribution is confirmed in writing before you pay, and only destinations we can name in advance are offered.

Is this news coverage?

It is a paid press release, written with you and published on The Dynamo Times, so it carries accurate, approved facts. We always present it as a paid release, which keeps it credible with the people who read it.

Will it push down negative results?

A release is one more accurate page about you, and it can appear in searches for your name. Search engines decide what they show, so no position or removal is promised.

How many releases do I get?

One release per order. Further releases are available as ongoing content where your agreement includes them.

Can I write it myself?

You can send a draft. We still check every fact and edit it to our standards before it is published.
