Your professional reputation goes beyond Google

Before a patient, client or board calls you, they look you up, often in several places at once. Each place draws on what is already published about you.

  • Google SearchRanks pages, and can show an AI summary above them.
  • Bing SearchRanks pages and feeds Microsoft's Copilot answers.
  • ChatGPT and other AI toolsAnswer questions about you from what is published online.
  • Professional directoriesHealthgrades, Avvo, state boards and bar listings.
  • Your name
  • Published articlesNews, press releases and interviews about your work.
  • Your own websiteThe one source you fully control.
  • Online reviewsOpinions from patients and clients.
  • Public profilesLinkedIn, employer pages and speaker pages.

Accurate, credible information has to be present in all of these places, and it has to agree.

What do search engines and AI tools say about your career?

Search engines list pages. AI tools write a short answer and name a few sources. Both can only work with what is published.

  • Sources agree: both have accurate material to show.
  • Sources disagree: an AI answer may hedge, repeat an old role or describe someone else.
  • Every month: we save what they say, with the date, so you can see the change.

We do not control Google, Bing or any AI tool, and no position or answer is guaranteed.

Your achievements deserve an accurate digital presence

The difference people notice is not volume. It is whether the sources agree.

When sources disagree

  • An old title on one site, a new one on another
  • A wrong city or a past employer shown as current
  • Achievements missing, or listed nowhere searchable
  • Little published in years

People hesitate, and AI tools hedge or get details wrong.

When sources agree

  • The same name, title and credentials everywhere
  • Current employer, city and specialty
  • Awards and publications listed with sources
  • Recent, dated material under your name

Searchers find one consistent story, and AI tools have accurate material to draw on.

The problem

People look you up before they call. You never hear what they found.

  • Old or one-sided resultsA story from years ago still sits near the top.
  • Someone else with your nameAnother doctor or lawyer is read as you.
  • Directories that disagreeOld addresses and past employers repeat everywhere.
  • A thin presenceDecades of work, one outdated staff page.
  • Reviews without contextA few strong opinions do all the talking.
  • AI answers that missChatGPT repeats an old role or knows nothing.

  • Digital visibility and SEOSearch optimization and better professional profiles, so accurate information is easier to find.

What can change, and what cannot

Knowing this first saves time and false hope.

  • Yours to change

    Quick, because you control it.

    • Your website
    • LinkedIn and claimable profiles
    • How your credentials are stated

  • Can be requested

    We ask with your documents. The site decides.

    • Directory and listing corrections
    • Updates to stories that moved on
    • Reviews that break a site's rules

  • Not ours to decide

    We add accurate context around it.

    • Accurate news reports
    • Reviews that follow the rules
    • How Google ranks and what AI tools say

When a result is negative

Three questions, in this order. The answers decide the work.

  1. 1Is it really about you?

    If not: mistaken identity

    We make you easy to tell apart: the same name, credentials, city and photo everywhere.

  2. 2Is it accurate?

    If not: false or wrong

    We ask the source to correct it, with your documents. Possible defamation goes to your attorney.

  3. 3Is it still current?

    If not: out of date

    We ask the publisher to update it, and publish the later outcome.

  4. Accurate, current and about you?

    We do not hide it. We help you reply where it fits, and publish accurate material about the rest of your work.

Questions people ask

Can you remove a bad review or article?

We can often help. A review that breaks a site's rules can be reported, and a publisher can be asked to correct or update a story. The site makes the final decision, so we are open about that, and we add accurate, current material so the full picture of your work is easy to find.

Will I reach the first page of Google?

Searches for your own name are where change is most realistic, because few pages compete for them, so that is where we focus first. Wider searches, such as a specialty in a city, take longer and depend on search engines, so we track them and report progress each month.

How long does this take?

What you control can change in days. Everything else depends on other sites and on search engines, and often takes months. We run the same searches again so you can see what moved.

Can you change what ChatGPT says about me?

We can influence it through the sources it reads. We correct the pages AI tools learn from and make your facts agree across them, then check the answers again. The tools update on their own schedule, and we save each month's answer so you can see the change.

How do you handle reviews?

With care. Replies stay short and general, never confirm that someone was a patient or client, and you approve each one. We report reviews that break a site's own rules, and we keep everything genuine: we never write, buy or arrange reviews.

Is it different for doctors and lawyers?

Yes, and we are set up for it. Patient privacy law and attorney confidentiality and advertising rules shape what can be said in public. We work within them, you approve every word, and legal advice stays with your own attorney.

What if the results are about someone with my name?

It can often be addressed. We state your full name, credentials, city and field the same way everywhere, so people and machines can tell the two of you apart.

Why do the packages include publishing?

Accurate material is how a reputation improves. Every package pairs reputation work with the publishing that supports it: your biography, your website and press releases, all approved by you.

Can I change package later?

Yes. You can move to a higher package. Your representative explains how billing changes before anything is charged.

How do I stop?

The terms, including how to cancel, are given to you in writing before you enroll.
