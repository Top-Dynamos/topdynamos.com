Philadelphia Trial Attorney Marcus Ellery Publishes a Free Guide for Families After Serious Car Crashes

The guide explains, in plain language, the first steps to take after a crash and the records to keep.

PHILADELPHIA, Pennsylvania, November 2, 2026

Send this releaseTextEmail

PHILADELPHIA, Pennsylvania, November 2, 2026. Marcus Ellery, a personal injury trial attorney in Philadelphia, has published a free guide for families in the first days after a serious car crash. The guide explains what to write down, which records to keep and which questions to ask, in language written for people rather than lawyers.

Mr. Ellery has represented injured people and their families for twenty years. He began his career as a judicial law clerk in the Court of Common Pleas in Philadelphia County, spent seven years at a civil trial firm, and founded Ellery Injury Law in 2015.

He is admitted in Pennsylvania and New Jersey and before the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. He teaches trial advocacy to newer lawyers and received the City Bar Pro Bono Program's service award in 2024.

The days after a crash are confusing, and families are asked to make decisions before they have the facts. This guide is meant to give them some order.

Marcus Ellery

Mr. Ellery has written fifteen articles for bar journals and course books, on subjects including depositions, medical records and opening statements, and has given about forty legal education talks.

The guide, his biography, admissions and published work are on his personal website.
