Questions people ask first

What does Top Dynamos do?

We write professional biographies from your documents, publish them as a personal website, a registry profile and press releases, and produce printed pieces where your package includes them. Reputation management is a separate service.

Who approves what is published?

You do. Nothing is published, printed or sent until you approve it in writing.

Is this an honor or a paid service?

A paid publishing service, with editorial checks. Paid recognition is always labeled as paid.

Do you promise search rankings?

We build what helps you most in search: accurate, well made pages under your name, so people find the right information. Search engines make the final call on order, so instead of promising a position we show you every month what changed.
