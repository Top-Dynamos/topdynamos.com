How we work

The same three rules for every client. Read our editorial standards.

  • Written from documents

    Every fact comes from the client's own records or a public source, and is checked before anything is written.

  • Approved before it goes live

    Nothing is published until the client has read it and approved it in writing.

  • Corrected when wrong

    If something we published is wrong, tell us and we review it. If permission is withdrawn, the profile comes down.
